# CourseHub (Team A - Advanced)

Pre-recorded e-learning platform built with Django.

## Sprints
1. Foundation, identity & storefront
2. Creator studio & curriculum management
3. Enrollment & digital classroom
4. Search, reviews & testing

## Curriculum management
Instructors can create courses from the Courses page, then add, reorder, edit,
or delete modules and lessons from each course. Lessons support notes and an
optional video URL. Curriculum changes are limited to the instructor who owns
the course.
